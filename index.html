Good to know

This form captures your request instantly, but a live company site needs a backend to actually process it. Before you launch, connect:

  • A booking API or database — to store reservations and check date availability.
  • A payment gateway — e.g. JazzCash, Easypaisa, or Stripe, to take deposits online.
  • Email/SMS confirmation — to send the itinerary and receipt automatically.
  • An admin view — so you can see and manage incoming bookings.
